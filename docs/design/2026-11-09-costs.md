## 1. Goal

Show a household what its energy costs: today so far (Live), per bucket and per period with a
comparison (History), and a forecast for the calendar year. Supports single-tariff, double-tariff
(normal/off-peak) and dynamic (quarter-hour) contracts, net metering until 2026 and the regime
without net metering from 2027.

A household can add **one comparison profile** (e.g. a dynamic contract next to its fixed one) to
see what it would have paid with that contract (§2.1).

Out of scope: comparing suppliers automatically, invoices/payments, CSV export.

## 2. Tariff model

Tariffs are stored per household (signature) as a list of **tariff periods**, each valid from a
date until the next one starts. Amounts are in euros; "incl." means including energy tax and VAT,
as on a Dutch annual statement.

```ts
TariffPeriod = {
  validFrom: 'YYYY-MM-DD';
  electricity:
    | { type: 'single'; perKwh: number }                          // incl.
    | { type: 'double'; normalPerKwh: number; offPeakPerKwh: number } // incl.
    | { type: 'dynamic'; markupPerKwh: number; energyTaxPerKwh: number; vatPercent: number };
      // dynamic: price = (quarter-hour day-ahead price + markup + energy tax) × (1 + VAT)
  export: {
    compensationPerKwh: number;   // feed-in compensation (excess under net metering; all export from 2027)
    feePerKwh: number;            // feed-in costs charged by the supplier, per exported kWh
    dynamicMarkupPerKwh?: number; // dynamic: export earns day-ahead price + this (may be negative)
  };
  gas?:
    | { type: 'fixed'; perM3: number }                            // incl.
    | { type: 'dynamic'; markupPerM3: number; energyTaxPerM3: number; vatPercent: number };
  fixed: {
    supplierPerMonth: number;     // vastrecht leverancier (electricity + gas), incl.
    gridPerMonth: number;         // netbeheerkosten (electricity + gas), incl.
  };
  taxReductionPerYear: number;    // heffingskorting, incl. VAT, as a positive amount
}
```

Validation: amounts ≥ 0 except markups (may be negative); VAT 0–100 %; `validFrom` unique;
periods sorted by date. Days before the first period have **no costs** (shown as "–").

### 2.1 Tariff profiles

A household has **one or two tariff profiles**:

| Profile                                                  | Use                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `own` ("Mijn contract")                                  | the costs shown everywhere  |
| `compare` (optional, with a name, e.g. "Dynamisch ANWB") | shown next to the own costs |

Each profile has its own list of tariff periods with valid-from dates, so a comparison profile can
start at the household's first data and recalculate the past ("what would 2025 have cost with a
dynamic contract?"). The cost engine is the same for both; only the tariff periods differ.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Bands for double tariffs

The kit only forwards the total counters (no T1/T2 split), so the band is derived from time:
**off-peak** = Monday–Friday 23:00–07:00 and all of Saturday and Sunday (local time); otherwise
**normal**. Usage per quarter hour is assigned to the band of that quarter hour. National
holidays are not treated as off-peak (grid operators differ); a known approximation.

### 3.2 Usage per quarter hour

Import, export and gas per 15-minute interval from the counters at the quarter boundaries
(`aggregateWindow(every: 15m, fn: last)`, deltas like the history buckets).

**Changed during implementation (#42):** use during a gap is spread evenly over the gap's quarters
instead of being left out, so totals match the counters (and a statement). Gaps longer than an hour
mark the day as `estimated`; shorter ones are normal (kits pause, gas meters report hourly).
Quarters after the last value are unknown.
Gas counters update every 5 minutes or hourly; for dynamic gas only the daily total matters.

### 3.3 Electricity costs from 1-1-2027 (no net metering)

Per quarter hour:

- import × price (single/double band, or dynamic: `(dayAhead + markup + energyTax) × (1+VAT)`)
- − export × compensation (single/double), or export × `(dayAhead + dynamicMarkup)` (dynamic)
- - export × feed-in fee

### 3.4 Electricity costs until 31-12-2026 (net metering), per calendar year

Export cancels import for as long as the year's cumulative import exceeds cumulative export:

**Decided during implementation (#42):** the feed-in fee (`feePerKwh`) applies to every exported
kWh in both regimes; suppliers already charge it under net metering, and users without one enter 0.
The year-end band netting of double tariffs is booked on 31 December, so days add up to the year.

- **Single tariff**: netted kWh cost nothing; per quarter hour `(import − export) × price` while
  the running year balance (import − export) stays ≥ 0. Once export exceeds import for the year,
  the excess earns `compensationPerKwh`.
- **Double tariff**: like single, but with two running balances: normal against normal and
  off-peak against off-peak first; a remaining surplus in one band nets against the other band's
  deficit at the end of the year (shown in the year totals; per-bucket values use the band
  balances).
- **Dynamic**: the **supply part** is not netted — import costs `dayAhead + markup`, export earns
  `dayAhead + dynamicMarkup` per quarter hour (excl. VAT). The **energy tax and VAT** are netted:
  energy tax is charged on `max(0, yearImport − yearExport)` kWh, VAT over the supply part plus
  that tax. Per bucket the tax part is shown with the running balance as for single tariffs.

Because net metering is settled per year, a bucket's cost is "as far as known now": with a running
balance that becomes negative later in the year, earlier buckets do not change retroactively.

### 3.5 Gas, fixed costs and tax reduction

- Gas: m³ × fixed price, or dynamic `(dayPrice + markup + energyTax) × (1+VAT)` per day.
- Fixed costs per day = (supplier + grid per month) / days in that month.
- Tax reduction per day = taxReductionPerYear / days in that year (subtracted).
- A bucket's **total cost** = electricity + gas + fixed − tax reduction (for hour buckets the
  fixed costs and reduction are spread per hour).

## 4. Prices

### 4.1 Sources

| Data                     | Source                                                       | Resolution                            | Notes                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Electricity day-ahead NL | ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (A44, area `10YNL----------L`) | 15 min since 1-10-2025, 60 min before | free API key (`ENTSOE_TOKEN`) |
| Gas day price            | EnergyZero public API (`usageType=3`)                        | day                                   | excl. VAT and tax             |

Prices are stored **excluding VAT** in EUR per kWh / per m³.

Found while building phase 1:

- ENTSO-E uses curve type **A03**: positions whose price equals the previous one are omitted and
  must be filled in by the parser.
- EnergyZero returns gas prices per hour (the price changes once a day), excl. VAT. **Resolved
  (#42):** they are wholesale day prices **without energy tax**: 2023–2026 values follow TTF
  (€ 0.26–0.54/m³) without jumps at the yearly tax changes, and the product owner confirmed that
  the current € 0.84/m³ matches a consumer price of ~€ 1.80/m³ only with energy tax added. The
  formula of §3.5 applies as designed.

### 4.2 Storage (InfluxDB, separate bucket)

Bucket `energy-prices` (created by the product owner, read/write token only for this bucket):

| Measurement             | Tags                                 | Field         | Time              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------- | ----------------- |
| `electricity_day_ahead` | `area=NL`, `resolution=PT15M\|PT60M` | `eur_per_kwh` | interval start    |
| `gas_day_ahead`         | `area=NL`                            | `eur_per_m3`  | day start (local) |

### 4.3 Ingestion

A price job in the API process (one instance):

- every day at 13:15 and 15:00 local (day-ahead results are published ~12:45–13:00) fetch
  tomorrow; at start-up and every hour fill any missing days of the last 7 days;
- one-time **backfill** from 2023-08-01 (first data) in chunks of 7 days, rate-limited;
- idempotent writes (same timestamp overwrites); failures logged, retried on the next run.
- **Changed during implementation (#36, #39):** each source (electricity, gas) runs on its own.
  A failing price source backs off (Retry-After, else 1 h doubling up to 24 h; health
  `backoff`). Errors of our own price database, such as write timeouts while InfluxDB makes its
  half-hourly backup, are retried after 10 minutes (health `retrying`). Chunks are 7 days
  instead of a month so that a write finishes within the 30 s timeout on a busy server.

## 5. Tariff storage (SQLite on the server)

- Built-in `node:sqlite` (no native dependency), file `/var/lib/p1-meter-app/p1.db`
  (systemd `StateDirectory=p1-meter-app`, owned by `p1meter`, survives deploys and rollbacks).
- Tables:
  - `tariff_sets(signature PRIMARY KEY, profiles JSON, version INTEGER, updated_at)` —
    `profiles = { own: { periods }, compare?: { name, periods } }`
  - `tariff_versions(signature, version, profiles JSON, created_at, client_ip_hash)` — every save
    keeps the previous set; the UI can restore any version (undo vandalism or mistakes).
  - `daily_costs(signature, profile, day, tariff_version, json)` — cache of computed daily costs
    (§6.2), invalidated when the tariffs change.
- Migrations: numbered SQL files applied at start-up in a transaction.
- Backup: daily `VACUUM INTO /var/lib/p1-meter-app/backups/p1-YYYY-MM-DD.db`, 14 kept.
- Editing: anyone with a valid session may save (product owner decision); writes are
  rate-limited (30/hour per household; every change in the Settings screen is a save) and logged with masked signature.

## 6. API

### 6.1 Endpoints (session required)

| Route                                      | Purpose                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/v1/tariffs`                      | current profiles (own, optional compare) + version                          |
| `PUT /api/v1/tariffs`                      | save profiles (optimistic: `If-Match: <version>`, `409` on conflict)        |
| `GET /api/v1/tariffs/versions`             | earlier versions (date, number of periods)                                  |
| `POST /api/v1/tariffs/versions/:n/restore` | restore a version (creates a new version)                                   |
| `GET /api/v1/costs/today` (new, §7.1)      | today's costs per profile, instead of extending `/live/today`               |
| `GET /api/v1/history` (extended, §7.1)     | `costs`: per bucket, totals, previous and the forecast (current month/year) |
| `GET /api/v1/costs/days?from=&to=`         | daily costs per profile (max. 366 days), reason when unknown (#42)          |

### 6.2 Calculation

- Cost engine as pure functions in `apps/api/src/costs/` (fully unit-tested), input: usage per
  quarter hour, prices, tariff periods, year-to-date netting balances.
- Daily costs are computed from quarter-hour usage and prices (one Flux query per day range,
  joining usage and prices) and cached in `daily_costs`; weeks, months and years sum days.
  Day views (hourly buckets) are computed directly.
- Net metering needs the running balance since 1 January: taken from the cached days of that year.

### 6.3 Forecast

`actual cost year-to-date + expected rest`: for the remaining days, the cost of the same days last
year (if available and complete), otherwise the average daily cost of the last 30 days. Shown with
"o.b.v. verbruik vorig jaar" or "o.b.v. de afgelopen 30 dagen".

### 7.1 Phase 4 details (#49)

Decisions by the product owner (2026-11-09):

| Topic           | Decision                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Amount shown    | Everything: electricity + gas + fixed costs − tax reduction (can be negative on sunny days) |
| Today (running) | Fixed costs and tax reduction pro rata to the elapsed part of the day                       |
| History         | Summary card, cost chart, and a forecast in the **month and year** views                    |
| Estimated days  | Small label "geschat"; lighter bars, as for gaps in the usage charts                        |

API (changes to §6.1):

- `GET /api/v1/costs/today` (new, instead of extending `/live/today`, so the live tiles stay
  fast): `{ configured, own, compare? }`. `configured` is false when no profile has a tariff
  period (→ hint). `own` and `compare.cost` are `{ totalEur, electricityEur, gasEur, fixedEur,
taxReductionEur, estimated }`, or `{ unknown: reason }`. Refreshed every minute.
- `GET /api/v1/history` (extended) gets `costs` (absent without any tariff period):
  - per bucket `{ own: number | null, compare?: number | null, estimated? }`;
  - `totals` and `previous` as `{ own, compare? }`; `previous` covers the same elapsed part as
    the usage comparison (whole days plus the elapsed fraction of the current day);
  - `compareName`;
  - `forecast` only for the **current** month or year: `{ own, compare?, basis }` with basis
    `last-year`, `last-30-days` or `mixed` (§6.3; per remaining day the same day last year when
    its cost is known, otherwise the average of the last 30 known days).
- The forecast is part of the history response instead of a separate `GET /costs/forecast`.
- Hour buckets (day view): electricity per quarter hour summed per hour; gas per hour at the
  day's unit price; fixed costs and tax reduction spread evenly over the hours of the day.
- Unknown buckets (no tariff, no price, no usage) are `null` and shown as "–"; a total over a
  period with unknown days is marked "onvolledig", like the usage totals.

Web:

- **Live**: tile "Kosten vandaag" below "Vandaag": the total, a second line for the comparison
  contract ("Met Dynamisch: € 2,71 · € 0,41 goedkoper", green when cheaper, warning colour when
  more expensive), and "geschat" when estimated. Tap → sheet with the breakdown (Stroom, Gas, Vaste
  kosten, Heffingskorting, Totaal) for both contracts. Without tariffs: "Vul je tarieven in om
  kosten te zien" with a link to Instellingen → Tarieven.
- **History**: summary card "Kosten" in the style of the usage cards (change versus the previous
  period; lower cost = green), with the comparison contract below it ("Met <naam>: € … (€ …
  goedkoper/duurder)"). In the current month or year: "Verwacht: € … (o.b.v. vorig jaar | de
  afgelopen 30 dagen)" for both contracts.
- **Cost chart** "Kosten (€)" below Stroom and Gas: bars for the own contract (navy; negative
  amounts below zero), a line with dots for the comparison contract (green), estimated buckets
  dimmed, unknown buckets empty; tooltip with both amounts and "geschat". Same swipe and zoom as
  the other charts.
