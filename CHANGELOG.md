# Changelog

All versions, newest first, with a one-sentence summary. The full notes are on the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/AvansETI/p1-meter-app/releases) page. Generated from `apps/web/src/releases/releases.ts`
by `pnpm changelog`; do not edit by hand.

## [0.15.0] - 2026-11-15

Release notes under About (with About as its own menu item on desktop), and feed-in is called "Teruglevering" in Dutch.

## [0.14.2] - 2026-11-15

Gas use during gaps in the readings is estimated from the meter readings and marked as estimated.

## [0.14.1] - 2026-11-15

The splash screen says "Updating…" as soon as an update is found and stays two seconds after it.

## [0.14.0] - 2026-11-15

Fuller festive decorations, with a piet throwing pepernoten and a livelier Movember, and a live indicator that beats like a heart.

## [0.13.1] - 2026-11-15

An open app notices a new version within seconds, and the splash screen no longer starts twice during an update.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-15

Gas analysis settings are shared between all devices of a household.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-15

Gas analysis with degree days, a settlement forecast and comparison with other households and CBS figures.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-15

Festive decorations all year round, with information about each holiday on tap.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-14

Christmas and New Year decorations, usage statistics and automatic updates.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-12

A language setting (Dutch or English), and the licenses open inside the app.

## [0.8.1] - 2026-11-10

Tariff changes show at once.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-10

A loading indicator, names for tariff periods and a switch for the comparison contract.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-09

Costs in the app: today's costs on Live, and costs with forecasts in History.

## [0.6.1] - 2026-11-09

Clearer meter information in Settings.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-09

Daily costs per tariff contract, and the MNEXT logo in the title bar.

## [0.5.1] - 2026-11-09

Prices are fetched more reliably when the price database is briefly unavailable.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-09

Tariffs per household under Settings, with versions and daily backups.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-09

Day-ahead electricity and gas prices are fetched and stored, as groundwork for costs.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-09

History per day, week, month and year, with electricity, gas and a comparison with the previous period.

## [0.2.3] - 2026-11-09

Charts on iOS appear at once, without an intro animation.

## [0.2.2] - 2026-11-09

A gas chart per hour on the Live page, and a fix for charts on iOS.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-09

Fixes from the first production deploy.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09

Automatic deployment to production at p1-meter.sendlab.nl.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

First release: an installable app with EMON ID entry and a live dashboard for electricity and gas.
